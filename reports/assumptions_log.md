# Assumptions Log: Collections Performance & Forensics Investigation

This document maintains an auditable, version-controlled record of all methodological, financial, and operational assumptions adopted throughout the analysis.

| Assumption ID | Category | Stated Assumption | Evidence / Operational Rationale | Sensitivity & Financial Impact | Audit Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ASM-001** | Payment Dedup | Identical `account_id` and `amount` within 10 minutes with status `FAILED`/`REVERSED` represents payment gateway retry noise. | Standard banking gateway retry loop; verified by duplicate transaction logs. | High (Direct impact on gross recovered cash). Tested across 10m/1h/24h. | Validated |
| **ASM-002** | Realized Cash | Only payments with `payment_status = 'SUCCESS'` represent realized cash recovery; `REVERSED` transactions are deducted from net cash. | Standard accounting standard for collections recognition. | Critical (Governs true recovery rate numerator). | Confirmed |
| **ASM-003** | PTP Kept Window | A PTP is classified as "KEPT" if a verified payment occurs within $\pm 3$ days of `promised_date` matching promised amount $\pm 10\%$. | Industry standard grace period for banking clearance and installment settlement. | High (Reconciled directly against `promises_to_pay.status`). | Active Test |
| **ASM-004** | Attribution Window | Payments are attributed to a collection touchpoint if the touchpoint occurred within 7 days prior to payment. | Standard multi-touch lookback window for consumer collections. | High (Governs channel efficiency & ROI). | Sensitivity Tested (1d / 3d / 7d / 14d) |
| **ASM-005** | Timeline Scope | Operational collection performance is evaluated over the 7 full months (Jan–Jul 2026); Aug 2026 is treated as an incomplete period (terminating Aug 8–12). | Raw event data terminates on 2026-08-08/12; full month comparisons require complete calendar boundaries. | Medium (Prevents artificial drop-off in Aug metrics). | Confirmed |
| **ASM-006** | Capital Horizon | The ₹10 Cr capital deployment is evaluated over a 12-month operational payback window with a zero-residual-value hurdle. | Conservative corporate finance standard for collections technology and operational scaling. | High (Defines required monthly incremental cash flow). | Confirmed |
| **ASM-007** | Missing Client Data | Client/Lender/Originator dimension is entirely unobserved in raw data (`accounts.csv` contains only `loan_type` and borrower linkage). | Confirmed by schema audit of all 17 CSV files and `data_dictionary.csv`. | High (Precludes origin-level institutional segmentation). | Formally Documented Gap |
| **ASM-008** | Missing Language Data | Direct borrower language preference is unobserved. `borrowers.state` and `city` serve as regional linguistic proxies. | Confirmed by schema audit of `borrowers.csv`. | Medium (Regional clustering used instead of direct language pairing). | Formally Documented Gap |
| **ASM-009** | Agent Identity Unreliable | There is no 1:1 human agent identity in the dataset. `agent_id` (1,000 unique) and `employee_code` (1,099 unique) cross-collide across 30,000 rows with multiple distinct names and vendors per ID. No combination of columns across `agents`, `agent_sessions`, or `calls` provides a 1:1 person-level mapping. Operational call metrics keyed on `agent_id` are usable in aggregate, but individual agent tenure, coaching, and individual performance cannot be reliably attributed to single real persons. | Confirmed by empirical cross-join and composite key checks across `agents.csv`, `agent_sessions.csv`, and `calls.csv`. | High (Precludes micro-level agent coaching and true individual tenure attribution). | Formally Documented Gap |
| **ASM-010** | Complaints Attribution | Complaints in `complaints.csv` have no direct `agent_id`, `vendor_id`, or `campaign_id`. They are attributed to the nearest preceding touchpoint for that account/borrower within a 14-day window. | Necessary due to missing foreign keys in `complaints.csv`. | High (Governs conduct scorecards by agent/channel). | Confirmed |
